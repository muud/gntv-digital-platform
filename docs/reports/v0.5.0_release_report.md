# GNTV DIGITAL v0.5.0 Release Report

Release date: 2026-07-20
Release status: **APPROVED AND RELEASED**
Release scope: CMS Module 4 — Editorial Workflow & Publishing

## Executive summary

GNTV DIGITAL v0.5.0 finalizes the approved CMS Module 4 backend. The release adds the Editorial domain without changing the established ownership boundaries of Content Core, Media Library, or Premium Catalog. Release-blocking PostgreSQL serialization, archived-workflow recovery, and Editorial OpenAPI contract issues are resolved. No Module 5 feature is implemented in this release.

## Features completed

- Ten-state editorial lifecycle covering draft, review, fact check, legal review, editorial approval, approval, scheduling, publication, expiry, and archive.
- Role-aware transitions, reporter/editor/producer assignments, reassignment, priorities, due dates, and optimistic locking.
- Comments, structured review notes, mentions, immutable activity history, revision snapshots, comparison, and non-destructive restore.
- Immediate and scheduled publication, embargo and unpublish planning, due-work processing, republish, and emergency unpublish.
- In-app, email-ready, and webhook-ready notification outbox records, reminders, publishing alerts, dashboards, and audit events.
- Privileged archived-workflow restore to `approved`, with obsolete publication planning cleared.
- JSON-safe Editorial activity and audit payloads for PostgreSQL datetime persistence.

## Known limitations

- A production downstream publisher adapter is not connected; notification outbox records are email/webhook ready but external delivery requires deployment integration.
- FFmpeg transcoding, adaptive bitrate HLS/DASH packaging, CDN publication, and full media delivery automation are not implemented.
- Production infrastructure credentials and live managed-service verification are not stored or executed in the repository.
- The configured 98.58% coverage gate is targeted, not whole-codebase coverage.
- Existing deprecation warnings should be resolved before major dependency upgrades.
- The untracked legacy/prototype application tree is outside the official release payload and is not production-approved by this release.

## Ready for Module 5

CMS Module 4 is approved and closed. GNTV DIGITAL is ready to begin Module 5 — Distribution & Geo-Fencing — under its separately approved scope. v0.5.0 contains no Module 5 routes, models, migrations, workers, tests, or feature claims.
